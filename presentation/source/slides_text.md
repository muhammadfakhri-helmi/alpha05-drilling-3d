### Notes:
PROJECT MANAGER: Go / No-Go is a simple rule. The spud is GO only if all of these are true: rig, services, contracts and materials are ready; permits, HSE and well-control bridging are closed; emergency arrangements and reporting templates are in place; function, pressure and alarm tests are accepted; communication checks and H2S and emergency drills are done. The spud is NO-GO if any of these is open: a permit, document or rig acceptance gap, any readiness area that is not closed, a test that is not accepted, or a drill that is not completed. In that case delay the spud, close the gap and review again. The Company Man and all discipline leads recommend; the PM or delegated operator authority authorizes. A section proceeds only when its hold points and critical inputs are closed.
